# Maple Court agreement

Fictional example for a software demonstration. These are not terms from a real lease.

## Rent
Monthly rent is $1,450.00, due on the first day of each month.

## Parking
One parking space is included at no additional charge.

## Notice before moving out
The resident must give written notice at least 30 days before moving out.

## Repairs
The owner will acknowledge a non-emergency repair request within 3 days.

## Guests
Overnight guests may stay for up to 14 days in a calendar month.

## Storage
The resident may use one storage locker at no additional charge.

## Pets
One cat is permitted with written approval.
