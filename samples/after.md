# Maple Court agreement

Fictional example for a software demonstration. These are not terms from a real lease.

## Rent
Monthly rent is $1,575.00, due on the first day of each month.

## Parking
One parking space is available for a monthly fee of $65.00.

## Notice before moving out
The resident must give written notice at least 60 days before moving out.

## Repairs
The owner will acknowledge a non-emergency repair request within 7 days.

## Pets
One cat is permitted with written approval.

## Guests
Overnight guests may stay for up to 7 days in a calendar month.

## Package service
A monthly package service fee of $12.00 applies to the unit.
